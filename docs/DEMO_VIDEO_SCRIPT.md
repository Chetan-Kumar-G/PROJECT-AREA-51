# 30-second demo video: script and shot list

**How to record:** use any screen recorder (Windows: press `Win + Alt + R` with Xbox Game Bar, or use the Snipping Tool's video mode). Open the live site in full screen at 1920×1080. Record each shot separately, then join them in Clipchamp (free on Windows). Read the narration slowly; it fits in 30 seconds.

| Time | What to show (on screen) | Narration (voice-over) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | Home page. Slowly scroll past "They stopped talking. Their stories didn't." | "Since 1958, NASA has left machines all over the solar system." |
| 4–9 s | Ghost Map → click **Zoom to the Apollo sites** → click Tranquility Base. The dossier opens with its ghost log. | "Ghosts of Exploration lets kids find them, and hear each one tell its own story." |
| 9–14 s | Scroll down in the dossier to **How it worked** → press ▶ on the Apollo landing animation. | "Step-by-step animations show how they landed, like Apollo's lunar module…" |
| 14–18 s | **How it worked** gallery → Sky crane, steps 3–5 (rover lowered on cables). | "…or the sky crane that lowered Curiosity onto Mars." |
| 18–21 s | **Then & now** slider: drag across Apollo 11 → the orbital view in 2009. | "They're still there today." |
| 21–25 s | **Land it yourself** (Mars level): cut the parachute and fire the thrusters until touchdown. | "Kids can even try landing one themselves, with real gravity…" |
| 25–28 s | Quick cuts: 3D Mars globe, the Curiosity 3D model spinning, the teacher pack worksheet. | "…explore in 3D, and learn in class." |
| 28–30 s | Home page title with the site address. | "Ghosts of Exploration: abandoned, but not forgotten." |

**Tips**
- Turn on the "Scientist" reading level beforehand if you want longer text on screen, or keep "Explorer" for younger viewers.
- To show a guaranteed landing in the game, practise the Mars level once. Cut the chute at about 500 m, then hold 🔥 when the falling speed goes above about 25 m/s.
- Add captions (Clipchamp → Text) so the video works with the sound off.
- Space Apps also accepts a slide deck: `pitch.html` on the live site has 7 slides. Press → to advance, or print it to PDF.
