Mini-game

Land it yourself

Every machine in our archive had to land without crashing, with no pilot on board. Now it's your turn. Touch down slower than 3 m/s, on the flat landing zone, before your fuel runs out.

How to play
  • Main engine: hold ↑, W or Space, or the button.
  • Steer sideways: ← → or A D, or the ◀ ▶ buttons.
  • Cut the parachute (Mars): C or the ✂ button.
  • P pauses, R restarts.
  • The numbers turn green when you're slow enough to land safely.
