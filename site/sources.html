How we check our facts

Sources

Every story in this archive is built from real mission records. This page lists, machine by machine, the documents its facts were checked against and the NASA photo records it shows. Links open the original pages at NASA and partner agencies.

Ghost logs and epitaphs are written in each machine's voice for storytelling, but every fact in them comes from these records. Machine sources were compiled by our team member GuruPrasannaa Manikandan for the Cosmic-Atlas project and checked on the dates shown.
